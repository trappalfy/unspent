## Credits

A credit is an SPL token on Solana with 6 decimals. One credit pays for $1 of inference at Unspent's prices. Credits don't expire.

The token has no freeze authority, no transfer fees and no transfer hooks. You can hold it, send it to anyone or sell it like any other token.

## Buy with USDC

On [/app/buy](/app/buy) enter an amount and sign one transaction. `buy_credits` moves that much USDC into the program's reserve and mints the same number of credits to your wallet.

- At least 1 credit and at most <Limit name="maxBuyPerIx" /> per purchase.
- No purchase fee. You pay Solana network fees and, the first time, rent for your credit token account and your API balance account (about 0.002 SOL each).
- By default the same transaction deposits the credits to your API balance. **Keep in wallet** skips the deposit.

The program caps credits in circulation at <Limit name="supplyCap" />.

## Buy with any token

Choose a token from your wallet. The app builds one transaction: a Jupiter swap from your token to USDC, `buy_credits` for the swap's minimum output, and the deposit. If the swap returns more than its minimum, the extra USDC stays in your wallet.

Before you sign, the app shows the rate, the minimum number of credits, the price impact and the route. Slippage is 0.5%, 1% or 2%. Unspent adds no fee to the swap. If Jupiter has no route for a token, buy with USDC.

## API balance

Credits on your API balance sit in the program's vault, an account controlled by the program, not by Unspent. The program keeps two numbers for each wallet:

- `balance`: your credits in the vault;
- `reserved`: the part held for running requests.

`available` is `balance` minus `reserved`. It is what requests can use and what you can withdraw.

## Deposit and withdraw

Deposit moves credits from your wallet to your API balance. Withdraw moves available credits back to your wallet in the same transaction.

Withdrawals don't need our approval: only your wallet signs. They work while purchases or the API are paused, and after a shutdown.

## Holds and their expiry

Each request holds its worst-case cost before it runs (see [Models & pricing](/docs/models#what-a-request-holds)). When it ends, the program burns the actual cost and releases the rest.

If a request never settles, for example because the gateway went down, its hold expires after <Limit name="reservationTtl" />. From then on anyone can release it with the program's `expire` instruction, and the credits return to `available`.

You don't need Unspent for this. [/app/balance](/app/balance) reads your holds straight from the program and releases expired ones in one transaction. A withdrawal can close expired holds in the same transaction too.

## Hold limit

The program caps how much one wallet can hold at once at <Limit name="maxReservedPerUser" />. You can set a lower limit for your wallet on [/app/balance](/app/balance). Requests over it get `429 too_many_in_flight`.

## Sell

Unspent doesn't buy credits back while the service runs: credits are sold for inference. To exit, sell them on a DEX or send them to someone who needs inference.

[/app/sell](/app/sell) withdraws from your API balance if needed and swaps credits to USDC through Jupiter in one transaction, when a market for credits exists.

## Shutdown and redemption

If Unspent shuts down, the admin calls the program's `shutdown` instruction. It takes effect at once and can't be undone:

- purchases, deposits and new holds stop;
- running requests still settle, holds still expire, withdrawals still work;
- `redeem` opens: anyone holding credits burns them and receives the same amount of USDC from the reserve, 1:1.

[/app/redeem](/app/redeem) withdraws your API balance and redeems everything in one transaction. Credits in any wallet can be redeemed, not only those bought by that wallet.
