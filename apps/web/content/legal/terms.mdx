## 3. Credits

- **What a credit is.** A credit is a token on Solana with 6 decimals. It is a prepaid means of paying for inference through the Unspent API: one credit pays for $1 of inference at the prices shown on the site and at `/v1/models`.
- **Purchases are final.** Credits are sold for inference. While Unspent operates, we don't buy credits back and don't refund purchases.
- **Transfers.** You may send credits to anyone or sell them to others, for example on a decentralized exchange. Unspent is not a party to those transactions and makes no promise about the price of credits on any market.
- **No expiry.** Credits don't expire.
- **Prices.** Inference prices follow the model provider's list prices plus our margin, and they may change. A request is priced at the prices in effect when it starts.
- **Reserve.** The USDC paid for credits is held in the program's reserve and leaves it when credits are burned to pay for inference, when they are redeemed after a shutdown, or when USDC above the credits in circulation is moved to the treasury. How this works is described in the [Docs](/docs/onchain).
